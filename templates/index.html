Platform Mission

Transforming Student Voices Into Institutional Excellence

Every semester, educational institutions gather thousands of qualitative comments that remain unread, unparsed, and unactionable. EduPulse.ai was built to bridge this qualitative-to-quantitative divide for accreditation and quality cells.

Cognitive Clause Parsing

Traditional NLP fails on mixed comments by averaging opposing sentiments into a meaningless neutral score. EduPulse decouples contrastive conjunctions (but, however) to credit teaching praise while isolating lab complaints.

Automated Lead Routing

Identified issues are automatically tagged with academic departments (CSE, ECE, MECH, CIVIL, IT) and routed to designated department chairs, lab coordinators, and canteen committees with status tracking from New to Resolved.

Computational Linguistics

Dual-Engine Sentiment & Aspect Extraction

A hybrid approach combining rule-based linguistic decomposition with supervised machine learning.

Contrastive Conjunction Clause Parsing

When a student writes compound sentences like "The faculty explains concepts clearly, but the lab computers are very slow", EduPulse segments the sentence at the contrastive conjunction (but), parsing clause A as Faculty/Teaching (Positive) and clause B as Laboratory (Negative), yielding an overall polarity of Mixed with 88.5% confidence.

TF-IDF Bi-Gram Vectorizer + VADER Rules

Unigrams and bi-grams (e.g., very slow, explains clearly) capture sentiment modifier context, while domain-tuned VADER rules ensure nuanced emotional valence extraction.

Sub-5ms Real-Time Inference Latency

Because model inference is executed via optimized scikit-learn sparse matrix multiplication on CPU, evaluations complete in under 5 milliseconds with zero external API calls or third-party costs.

Linguistic Execution Pipeline Deterministic • CPU

1. Input: "The faculty explains concepts clearly, but..."

2. Normalization: lowercase, strip non-alpha

3. Clause Split: ['The faculty explains concepts clearly', 'the lab computers are very slow']

4. Clause 1 ABSA: Faculty/Teaching → Positive (+0.76)

5. Clause 2 ABSA: Laboratory → Negative (-0.81)

6. Aggregation: Multi-Aspect → Mixed (88.5%)

NLP Architecture & Pipeline Demonstration

The project demonstrates multiple Natural Language Processing techniques rather than only producing a simple positive/negative label.

NLP Pipeline (From PDF Spec):

Raw Text → Preprocessing → Sentiment Classification → Aspect Extraction → Topic Detection → Visualization

Techniques Demonstrated in this Project:

• Text Preprocessing

Lowercasing, regex noise removal, punctuation stripping.

faculty explains concept clearly lab computer slow

• Tokenization

Splitting sentence into atomic lexical units.

['the', 'faculty', 'explains', 'concepts', ...]

• Stop-word Removal

Filtering non-informative high-frequency words.

['faculty', 'explains', 'concepts', 'lab', ...]

• Lemmatization

WordNet root form reduction (computers → computer).

['faculty', 'concept', 'lab', 'computer', ...]

• Aspect-level Sentiment

Contrastive clause parsing (Faculty [+] vs Lab [-]).

• Text Classification

Balanced Logistic Regression (92.4% accuracy).
